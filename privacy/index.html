Privacy Policy

Dave | IT Guy Life · "Dave Publisher" app · Last updated: September 24, 2026

What this is

"Dave Publisher" is a private tool used only by the creator of the Dave | IT Guy Life channel to upload our own original videos to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

When the channel owner signs in, the platform gives the tool an access token for the channel owner's own account, plus basic account information (such as the channel or account name) to confirm the right account is connected. The tool uses this only to upload videos and their titles, descriptions and captions.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of viewers or any other people. The tool does not read other users' profiles, messages or videos. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, are never shared with third parties, and can be revoked at any time in the Google, TikTok or Meta account settings.
